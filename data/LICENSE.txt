Changes made: one entry is kept per word, pinyin is written with tone marks, senses are
shortened, and cross-references are removed. See tools/build_dictionary.py.

radicals.json is derived from dictionary.txt of Make Me a Hanzi
(https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi), the project the stroke data comes from.
See that project's COPYING file for its license terms. Changes made: only each
character's radical and each radical's first pinyin and English meaning are kept, and a
few radical forms without a meaning were filled in. See tools/build_radicals.py.
